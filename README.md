# ⚡ Powershine Tech — Industrial Electronics & Automation

A professional business website developed for **Powershine Tech**, a Tiruppur-based supplier and service provider specializing in industrial electronic products, machinery spares, repairs, calibration, and technical support.

## 🌐 About Powershine Tech

**Powershine Tech**, established in 2020, provides a range of industrial electronic products and services, including LCD displays, PLCs, VFDs, industrial modules, loom displays, repairs, calibration, and technical support.

The company serves customers looking for industrial electronic spares, automation components, and repair/support services.

### Key Products & Services

* Tsudakoma Loom Displays
* Tsudakoma Loom Display Touch Screens
* Digital LCD Displays
* Siemens PLCs
* Fongs Modules
* Setex Display Services
* Setex PLC Services
* VFDs
* Delta VFDs
* Altivar Machine / ATV320
* Analog Modules
* Industrial Electronic Repairs
* VFD & Servo Drive Repairs
* Inverter Repairs
* Test Equipment Repair & Calibration
* Technical Support

The company specifically highlights repair, calibration, and technical support for industrial electronic equipment.

# 💻 Website Project

This project is a modern, responsive business website designed to provide customers with:

* Company information
* Product browsing
* Product details
* Service information
* Enquiry management
* Contact information
* Customer enquiry/cart functionality
* Responsive experience across desktop, tablet, and mobile

## 🎯 Project Objective

The main objective of the website is to create a professional digital presence for Powershine Tech and make it easier for customers to:

1. Explore available industrial products.
2. Understand the services offered.
3. View product details.
4. Add products to an enquiry cart.
5. Submit product enquiries.
6. Contact the company directly.

# ✨ Main Features

## 🏠 Home Page

The homepage provides:

* Company introduction
* Featured products
* Services
* Business highlights
* Call-to-action sections
* Contact information

## 📦 Product Management

The website provides product-focused sections where customers can:

* View available products
* View product images
* Read product information
* Check product categories
* Add products to an enquiry/cart

## 🛒 Enquiry Cart

Instead of directly purchasing products, customers can add required products to an **Enquiry Cart**.

The customer can:

1. Select a product.
2. Add it to the enquiry cart.
3. Review selected products.
4. Submit an enquiry.
5. Provide contact details.
6. Send the enquiry to the company.

This makes the website suitable for a B2B industrial-products business where customers may need quotations or technical discussions before purchasing.

## 📩 Enquiry Management

Customer enquiries are collected through the website and can be forwarded to:

**Email:** [powershine07@gmail.com](mailto:powershine07@gmail.com)

The enquiry system helps the business manage customer requirements and follow up with potential customers.

## 🔐 Admin Management

The website includes a separate administration concept for managing business information.

Admin functionality can include:

* Product management
* Product image management
* Product information updates
* Stock information
* Enquiry management
* Website content management

The admin area is intended to remain separate from the normal customer-facing website.

## 📱 Responsive Design

The website is designed to work across:

* 💻 Desktop

The UI adapts to different screen sizes while maintaining usability and readability.
